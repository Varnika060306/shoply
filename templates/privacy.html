Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored in hashed form). As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment card details or bank account credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to authenticate you, to record and display your expenses, and to generate summaries and filters of your spending. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and is accessible only to your account. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may delete your expenses or your account at any time, after which your data will be removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser information when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team at support@spendly.example. We may update this policy from time to time and will revise the "Last updated" date when we do.
